## Brand & Style

The design system for Ipial Abogados is built on the pillars of **Authority, Tradition, and Modern Precision**. It targets a clientele seeking high-stakes legal representation, where trust is non-negotiable. The visual language balances the weight of legal history with the clarity of contemporary practice.

The chosen style is **Modern Corporate with Minimalist leanings**. It utilizes expansive white space to denote "room for thought" and clarity of mind. Drawing inspiration from the provided logo, the system incorporates thin, precise divider lines and a structured grid to reflect the meticulous nature of legal work. The atmosphere is quiet, professional, and high-trust, avoiding trendy clutter in favor of enduring elegance.

## Colors — Lex Imperial

The palette is anchored by **Forest Deep #05261D** and **Primary #00261b** — deep, almost-black greens that signify stability, heritage, and institutional authority. The primary-container **#0b3d2e** provides tonal layering for headers and elevated surfaces.

- **Primary:** Forest Deep (#00261b / #05261D) — key branding moments, primary buttons, authoritative headers. Container #0b3d2e for tonal depth.
- **Structure:** Platinum Silver **#B8B9BA** for 1px dividers, card borders, and subtle backgrounds. Charcoal Text **#2C3E50** for high-contrast copy.
- **Accent:** Teal Accent **#148F77** strictly for CTAs, hover borders, and active states — visibility without breaking professional decorum.
- **Neutrals:** Surface scale #f8f9f9 → #ffffff → #edeeee for page, cards, and tonal layering. Reduces eye strain vs pure white, maintains "premium paper" feel.

## Typography

High-contrast pairing bridging tradition and modern accessibility.

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Classic serif evoking historical weight of legal documents and courtroom prestige. All major headings and editorial moments.
- **Body & UI (Hanken Grotesk):** Sharp contemporary sans-serif for exceptional legibility in dense legal copy.
- **Labels:** Uppercase Hanken Grotesk with increased letter spacing to mimic "Specialized Legal Services" logo styling.

## Layout & Spacing

Fixed-grid system for desktop, fluid for mobile.

- **Rhythm:** Base 4px unit. Section padding 80px, gap 80px — airy, minimalist.
- **Grid:** 12-column, 1200px max width. Content blocks span 6–8 columns for readable line lengths.
- **Dividers:** Thin 1px horizontal Platinum Silver (#B8B9BA) lines separating logical sections instead of heavy background changes.
- **Mobile:** Margins 20px, gutters 16px, section padding 48px.

## Elevation & Depth

Minimalist, professional — avoids heavy shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth via subtle background shifts (surface-container-low/low/high) rather than shadow stacking.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Buttons and cards use 1px Platinum Silver borders.
- **Ambient Focus:** Raised elements (floating WhatsApp, modals) use soft diffused shadow tinted with primary green (0 12px 32px rgba(0,38,27,0.10)) — warm, integrated.

## Shapes

Soft rounding **0.25rem (4px)** default. Large layout sections remain sharp-edged for architectural quality. Inputs, buttons, cards: 0.25rem. Pills and avatars: 9999px.

## Motion — Imperial Polish

Subtle, performant — CSS-only + IntersectionObserver (<5kb JS).

- **Cards:** Hover lift translateY(-4px), shadow 0 12px 32px rgba(0,38,27,0.10), border-color teal-accent. Transition 250ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) — only transform, opacity, border-color.
- **Buttons:** Lift + soft scale on hover, shadow.
- **Images:** Scale 1.03 inside overflow-hidden rounded-lg on parent hover.
- **Underline motif:** ::after width 40px → 80px on parent hover, 300ms ease.
- **Nav links:** Sliding underline via ::after width transition.
- **Section reveals:** .reveal opacity 0 + translateY(16px) → opacity 1 + translateY(0) over 600ms ease, stagger via data-reveal-delay. IntersectionObserver threshold 0.15, rootMargin 0 0 -40px, unobserve after reveal. Respects prefers-reduced-motion — disables transforms and reveals.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary solid Primary #00261b with white Hanken Grotesk; hover lift + teal-accent border/shadow. Secondary: Platinum Silver border with Forest Deep text.
- **Cards:** White (surface-container-lowest) background, 1px Platinum Silver border, rounded 0.25rem, shadow-sm; hover lift + teal accent.
- **Chips/Tags:** Light Silver background with Charcoal text, pill shape.
- **Lists:** Thin horizontal Forest Green dashes echoing logo line work.
- **Dividers:** 1px Platinum Silver horizontal/vertical for columnar structure.
