## Brand & Style

The design system for Ipial Abogados is built on the pillars of **Authority, Tradition, and Modern Precision**. It targets a clientele seeking high-stakes legal representation, where trust is non-negotiable. The visual language balances the weight of legal history with the clarity of contemporary practice.

The chosen style is **Modern Corporate with Minimalist leanings**. It utilizes expansive white space to denote "room for thought" and clarity of mind. Drawing inspiration from the provided logo, the system incorporates thin, precise divider lines and a structured grid to reflect the meticulous nature of legal work. The atmosphere is quiet, professional, and high-trust, avoiding trendy clutter in favor of enduring elegance.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Forest Deep**, a rich green that signifies stability and growth. This is complemented by a sophisticated scale of grays that transition from dark **Charcoal** for text to **Platinum Silver** for interactive elements and subtle backgrounds.

- **Primary:** Forest Deep (#0B3D2E) is used for key branding moments, primary buttons, and authoritative headers.
- **Secondary:** Charcoal and Silver provide the structural foundation.
- **Accent:** A brighter Teal is reserved strictly for Call-to-Action (CTA) elements to ensure visibility without breaking the professional decorum.
- **Neutral:** A very light, cool-toned gray is used for page backgrounds to reduce eye strain compared to pure white, maintaining a "premium paper" feel.

## Typography

This system uses a high-contrast typographic pairing to bridge the gap between tradition and modern accessibility.

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** A classic serif that evokes the historical weight of legal documents and courtroom prestige. It should be used for all major headings and editorial moments.
- **Body & UI (Hanken Grotesk):** A sharp, contemporary sans-serif chosen for its exceptional legibility in dense legal copy.
- **Labels:** Set in uppercase Hanken Grotesk with increased letter spacing to mimic the "Specialized Legal Services" styling found in the firm's logo.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Fixed-Grid System** for desktop to ensure content remains centered and focused, transitioning to a fluid model for mobile devices.

- **Rhythm:** A base 4px unit governs all spacing. Section heights are generous (80px+) to maintain an airy, minimalist feel.
- **Grid:** A 12-column grid is used for desktop (1200px max width). Content blocks typically span 6 or 8 columns to keep line lengths readable.
- **Dividers:** Inspired by the logo, thin 1px horizontal lines (Platinum Silver) are used to separate logical sections instead of heavy background changes.
- **Mobile:** Margins scale down to 20px, and gutters to 16px. Section padding reduces to 48px to maintain momentum on smaller screens.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a minimalist and professional profile, this system avoids heavy shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily communicated through subtle shifts in background color (e.g., a slightly darker gray for a footer or sidebar).
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Buttons and cards use thin 1px borders in Forest Deep or Silver rather than drop shadows.
- **Ambient Focus:** When an element must appear "raised" (like a floating contact button or a modal), a very soft, diffused shadow with 5% opacity is used, tinted with the primary Forest Green to keep the shadow feeling integrated and "warm."

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft (0.25rem)**. 

While the legal field often uses sharp corners to denote "edge" and "strength," a subtle rounding of 4px provides a more approachable, modern feel without appearing overly "bubbly" or casual. This applies to buttons, input fields, and card containers. Large layout sections remain sharp-edged to maintain a structural, architectural quality.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are solid Forest Deep with white Hanken Grotesk text. Secondary buttons use a Silver border with Forest Deep text. Hover states involve a subtle darken of the background or a transition to the Teal accent for CTAs.
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist design with only a bottom border (1px) that thickens and turns Forest Deep upon focus. Labels remain small and uppercase above the field.
- **Cards:** Used sparingly for "Practice Areas." They feature a white background, a 1px Platinum Silver border, and no shadow. The header within the card is always set in EB Garamond.
- **Chips/Tags:** Used for legal categories (e.g., "Civil," "Administrative"). These use a light Silver background with Charcoal text, maintaining a neutral, organized appearance.
- **Lists:** Bullet points are replaced with thin horizontal Forest Green dashes to echo the line work in the brand logo.
- **Dividers:** Vertical dividers (0.5pt to 1pt) are used to separate navigation items or sidebar content, ensuring a clean, columnar structure.
